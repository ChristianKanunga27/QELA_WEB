Qela Agritech is a digital agriculture platform that uses technology to help farmers, agribusinesses and agricultural organizations manage farm information, records and agricultural activities more efficiently.

Qela Agritech is designed for farmers, cooperatives, agribusinesses, agricultural organizations, field officers and other stakeholders across the agricultural value chain.

Qela Agritech can help manage farmer profiles, farm information, crop activities, production records, agricultural inputs, harvest information and other important farm records.

Qela Agritech provides digital solutions including Agricole, Agrisasa and E-SACCOS, designed to support different agricultural and financial activities within the agricultural value chain.

Qela Agritech helps organize agricultural information digitally, improve record management, provide better visibility of farm activities and support more informed decisions across the agricultural value chain.
