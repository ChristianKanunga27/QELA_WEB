About Us

Rooms built around how people actually live — considered, unhurried, and made to be used.

How it started

Working since 2017, one room at a time

We started as a two-person cabinet shop with more sawdust than furniture. Three decades on, the tools have changed, but the instinct hasn't: build things that earn their place in a home.

Good design isn't decoration — it's a room that finally makes sense.

Today our studio works across residential and light commercial projects, pairing custom joinery with sourced pieces from makers we trust.
