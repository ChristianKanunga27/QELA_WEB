AgriCoop

Cooperative Management Platform

AgriCoop is a digital solution designed to simplify agricultural cooperative management. It helps cooperatives organize farmer information, manage members, monitor activities and maintain reliable operational records.

Member Management

members are grouped into several categories and are assigned roles to perform according to their resprctive responsibilities.

Farmer Records

Organizes farmer and agricultural data, farmers' full name, location, mobile number and bank account details are stored.

Reports & Analytics

Access useful reports and critical analysis as Cooperatives, Amcos and Farmers details are available, details like the number of Amcos or farmers in a region or district and financial budget of the year.

Operations

Streamline everyday activities example request to travel, request to buy equipments like computers, showing benefictiaries activities AMCOS, TRA and Wakaguzi.

AgriSasa

Digital Connections
