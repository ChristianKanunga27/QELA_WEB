About Us

Who We Are

QELA Technologies (T) Ltd is a Tanzanian technology company creating practical digital solutions for agriculture, businesses and organizations. We use technology to simplify everyday challenges, improve access to information and help people work more effectively.

Our work brings together digital systems, data management, web and mobile applications, and innovative technology to create solutions that are relevant to the people and organizations we serve. We focus on understanding the problem first, then building technology that is simple, reliable and useful.

From managing information and business operations to supporting agriculture and digital transformation, our goal is to turn technology into something people can actually use, understand and benefit from.
